The RAG Advantage

Why RAG Makes the Difference

ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI tools are incredibly powerful — but they only know what they were trained on. They've never read YOUR textbook.

🤖

Generic AI

  • Tools like ChatGPT and Claude generate answers from general training data
  • They can't reference your specific textbook, lecture slides, or study guide
  • Answers may be correct in general but wrong for YOUR exam
  • No way to verify which page or chapter the answer came from
📚

LearnSage RAG

  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) searches YOUR uploaded documents first
  • Every answer is grounded in your actual study material
  • Citations show exactly which page and chapter each answer comes from
  • If the content isn't in your material, LearnSage tells you — no hallucination

The result? Study content you can trust, built from the material you'll actually be tested on.
